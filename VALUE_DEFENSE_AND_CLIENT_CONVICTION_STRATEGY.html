3. WORD-FOR-WORD OBJECTION HANDLING SCRIPTS

Objection 1: "Why should I pay $20,000+ when another guy offered $3,000?"

> Your Response:

> "If your only goal is to have a website address on a business card, the $3,000 option is fine. But if your goal is to dominate Google search, convert cold ad traffic, and handle high-volume sales without crashing, a $3,000 website will break.

>

> A $3,000 freelancer installs a basic template with 25 heavy plugins. It loads in 5 seconds, loses half your ad visitors before they even see a product, and renders blank HTML to Google crawlers so you never rank organically.

>

> Our engineering team built a custom React 19 headless engine with static pre-rendering, automated Google Merchant syncing, and 1-click WhatsApp checkout tailored specifically for high conversion. We aren't selling you web pages; we are delivering an enterprise revenue engine."

Objection 2: "Can you break down the hours so I can see why it costs this much?"

> Your Response:

> "We don't quote like junior freelancers charging by the hour, because hours reward slow work and penalize expertise. A junior dev takes 80 hours to build a slow, insecure checkout; a master engineer builds a 0.6-second custom WhatsApp COD engine in 15 hours because of years of R&D.

>

> You are paying for proprietary build automation scripts, advanced JSON-LD structured data architecture, automated RSS feeds for Google Shopping, and zero-cost serverless deployment that saves you $2,000 a year in Shopify app fees forever.

>

> We charge for the commercial value, speed, and competitive monopoly this system gives your business."

Objection 3: "What if I don't need all these advanced SEO and pre-rendering features right now?"

> Your Response:

> "If you launch without static pre-rendering (prerender.cjs) and rich JSON-LD schemas today, Google will index your React single-page application as empty pages. Six months from now, when you want organic sales, you will have to scrap the entire website and rebuild it from scratch.

>

> We build it enterprise-grade from Day 1 so every dollar you spend on marketing compounds over time."
